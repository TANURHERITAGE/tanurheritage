About

Who we are

The Tanur Heritage is a cultural and historical program aimed at unveiling the rich history and vibrant traditions of Tanur, a coastal town in Kerala. The initiative brings together historians, scholars, and the local community to celebrate and explore Tanur's diverse cultural past, including its ancient maritime trade connections, historical landmarks, and unique traditions.

Through exhibitions, talks, and cultural performances, Tanur Heritage highlights the town's role in Kerala's historical narrative, showcasing its contributions to art, religion, and trade. The program seeks to preserve and promote awareness of Tanur's heritage for future generations.
