Tanur 2K26 Carnival Highlights

Our Programmes

Join distinguished historians, cultural dignitaries, researchers, artists, and community voices across our five signature carnival streams.

01

RESEARCH CONCLAVE

Academic Historiography & Manuscript Studies

A premier scholastic conference bringing together eminent university researchers, historians, and archivists. Focuses on peer-reviewed paper presentations regarding Tanur's ancient maritime port connections, Vettathunad royal lineage, regional manuscripts, Arab-Portuguese encounters, and indigenous Malabar historiography.

Dec 11 • Day 1 Academic • Papers • Panels Register for Conclave
02

ANGADI SORA

Community Dialogue, Oral Folklore & Living Memories

An open-air conversational gathering reviving the authentic tradition of “Sora” (nostalgic community dialogue). Elders, trade veterans, artisans, and storytellers recount living memories of the historical Tanur market (Angadi), ancient maritime commerce, port-side interactions, and local heritage legends.

Dec 12 • Day 2 Oral History • Bazaar Dialogues Register for Angadi Sora
03

GREEN SUMMIT

Ecological Heritage, Coastal Protection & Sustainability

A dedicated forum deliberating on the environmental heritage of Tanur's coastline and waterways. Experts in ecology, marine conservation, climate resilience, and sustainable development address coastal soil preservation, mangrove ecosystems, canal rejuvenation, and responsible eco-tourism.

Dec 13 • Day 3 Ecology • Coastal Resilience Register for Green Summit
04

MEET THE INFLUENCERS

Digital Storytelling, Youth Media & Cultural Promotion

An interactive summit bringing together digital content creators, documentary filmmakers, authors, social media storytellers, and visual artists. Explores creative strategies to document, promote, and amplify regional history, monuments, and cultural traditions to a global digital audience.

Dec 13 • Day 3 Creators • Media • Storytelling Register for Influencers Meet
05

GRAND FINALE

Valedictory Ceremony, Cultural Gala & Festival Honors

The grand celebratory culmination of Tanur 2K26 Heritage Carnival. Features distinguished public dignitaries, keynote valedictory addresses, traditional folk and cultural performances, announcement of heritage research awards, and the official release of festival proceedings.

Dec 14 • Day 4 Awards • Folk Arts • Valedictory Register for Grand Finale
